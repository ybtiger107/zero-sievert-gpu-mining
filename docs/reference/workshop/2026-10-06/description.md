# 💻 GPU Mining System – Passive BTC Economy

### *Rare hardware. Time investment. Market risk.*

---

## ⚠️ Important – Read Before Using

- **Mining Rig is NOT a hideout module.** It is a normal item.
- **Do NOT drop the Rig on the ground.** Some players reported it may become unpickable or lost. Keep it in your Inventory/Stash.
- **Mining progress is based on accumulated in-game play time while your rig level is active.**
- When BTC becomes ready, collect it before the next status update or it may disappear.

If you just obtained your first rig, re-enter the hub once to initialize its interaction.

---

## 🔧 What This Mod Adds

- **GPU Mining Rig** – Rare heavy item
- **Graphics Cards (GPU)** – Upgrade mining level
- **Bitcoin (BTC)** – Generated after sufficient play time
- **BTC USB** – Fixed-value risk control item
- **Dynamic BTC Price System**
- **Visual Feedback** – Rig sprite updates when BTC is ready

---

## 🛠 How Mining Works

- Loot a Mining Rig and keep it safely in your Stash.
- Insert GPUs via Craft Table (Extras).
- While your rig level is active, mining progress accumulates through normal gameplay.
- After a full cycle completes, BTC becomes available.
- Loot BTC and decide whether to sell or secure it.

Mining does not require placing the rig in the hideout.

---

## 💰 BTC Price & Risk System

- BTC starts at a base value.
- After each completed mining cycle, its price randomizes within a range.
- Higher price = higher reward.
- Lower price = market loss.

This creates a risk-based economy.

---

## 🔐 BTC USB – Risk Control Tool

BTC USB converts 1 BTC into a fixed-value asset.

- Recipe: 1 BTC + 1 USB (Extras crafting)
- Fixed value: 250,000
- Prevents further price fluctuation

Use USB when:

- You mined at a low price and want stability
- You prefer guaranteed value over risk
- You want to secure profit before the next mining cycle

It is not always the highest profit —
it is the safest choice.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is the Mining Rig a module?**
No. It is an item.

**Does it work in storage?**
Yes. Keep it in Inventory/Stash. Do not drop it.

**Why is nothing happening?**
Ensure:

- Your rig level is active
- GPUs are inserted
- You have accumulated enough in-game play time

**Can I find BTC without mining?**
Yes. BTC can appear as rare loot.

**Other mods breaking it?**
Possible conflicts if other mods modify item/script systems.
